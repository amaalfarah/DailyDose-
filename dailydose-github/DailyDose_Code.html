Medication Tracking Disclaimer
This app is designed to help users track medications, schedules, and reminders only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose medical conditions, or replace consultation with a licensed healthcare provider. Always follow the instructions provided by your doctor, pharmacist, or prescription label.
Missed Dose Warning
If you miss a dose, consult your pharmacist or doctor before taking extra medication.
Emergency Warning
Do not use this app for emergency medical decisions. In case of a medical emergency, contact emergency services or a healthcare provider immediately.
Notification Warning
Reminder alerts may fail if phone notifications are disabled, battery-saving settings are active, or device connectivity is interrupted. Users are responsible for ensuring notifications remain enabled.
User Responsibility
You are responsible for entering accurate medication information, dosage schedules, and reminder settings.
Privacy Notice
The App is a standalone consumer tool and is not a "Converted Entity" or "Business Associated" as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Consequently, the health information you input is not "Protected Health Information" (PHI) governed by HIPAA. Instead, your data is protected under our standard Privacy Policy and applicable consumer protection laws, such as FTC's Health Breach Notification.
